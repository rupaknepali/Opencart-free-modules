Age Verification for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-age-verification/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
An "Are you 18 or older?" popup on the first visit, remembered on that device for the days you set.
This is an age gate, not legal age verification: check the law for your products and country.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload ageverify.ocmod.zip, click Install), then Extensions > Extensions > Modules:
install Age Verification, Edit, turn Status on and save.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
