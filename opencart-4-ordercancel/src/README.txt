Customer Order Cancellation for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
Lets logged-in customers cancel their own order from Account > Order History while it is still in a
status you choose (e.g. Pending, Processing) and inside a time limit. Asks for a reason, sets the
order to Canceled through the normal order history (customer email, restock) and alerts the owner.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload ordercancel.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions >
Modules > Customer Order Cancellation > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-customer-order-cancellation/
