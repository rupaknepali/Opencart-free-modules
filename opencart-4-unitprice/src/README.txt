Unit Price for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
Shows the price per kg, 100 g, litre, 100 ml, metre, m², oz, lb, fl oz or piece under the price
on product pages and product cards.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload unitprice.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
Unit Price > Install and Edit. Set each product's content on its Unit Price tab.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-unit-price/
