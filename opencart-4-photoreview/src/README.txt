Photo Reviews for OpenCart 4
============================
Customers add up to 3 photos to a product review. Photos are checked, resized to 1600 px and
re-saved as JPEG (removing EXIF data such as GPS location), and appear only once you approve the
review. Each review shows its photos, page 1 of the reviews shows a "Customer photos" gallery,
and photos open in a lightbox. In Catalog > Reviews, the review form shows its photos with
delete buttons; deleting a review deletes its photos.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload photoreview.ocmod.zip and click the green + button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install "Photo Reviews".
3. Edit: turn Status on and save. Reviews must be on in System > Settings > Option.

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-photo-reviews/
