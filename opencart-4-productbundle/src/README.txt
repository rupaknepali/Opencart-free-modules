Product Bundles for OpenCart 4
==============================
"Buy these together and save 10%": bundles of 2 to 6 products with a percentage discount,
offered on each product page and applied in the cart and at checkout as an order total.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload productbundle.ocmod.zip and click the green + button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Order Total: install "Product Bundles".
3. Edit: add your bundles, turn Status on and save.

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-bundles/
