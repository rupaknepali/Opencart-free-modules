Product Video for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-video/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).

Adds YouTube or Vimeo videos to product pages, in a Video tab or under the main image. The player
loads only when the shopper clicks play (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embed), so pages stay fast.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload productvideo.ocmod.zip, then click the green Install button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Product Video (creates the oc_product_video table).
3. Edit: add videos (product + YouTube/Vimeo URL), turn Status on and save.

Uninstalling keeps the table. To remove it: DROP TABLE oc_product_video;
