Newsletter Popup for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
A sign-up popup that opens after a delay or on exit intent, with a consent checkbox, optional
double opt-in email, a coupon code shown after sign-up and a CSV export of subscribers.
On phones it opens as a bottom sheet. Checkout and account pages never show it.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload newsletterpopup.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions >
Modules > Newsletter Popup > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-newsletter-popup/
