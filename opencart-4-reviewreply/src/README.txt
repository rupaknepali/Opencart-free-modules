Review Replies for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
Reply to product reviews from Catalog > Reviews. The reply shows under the review on the product
page, and reviewers with a customer account can get an email. The settings page lists approved
reviews that still have no reply, lowest rating first.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload reviewreply.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions >
Modules > Review Replies > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-reply-to-reviews/
