Mega Menu for OpenCart 4
========================
Replaces the top category menu with wide panels: subcategories in up to 4 columns, their own
subcategories underneath ("More…" after the limit), and a featured product (image, name, price
or special) or the category image on the right. Hover or keyboard focus opens a panel on desktop
(Escape closes it); on phones each category has a button that expands its panel. Extra links
(Specials, Brands, Blog...) can be added after the categories. The menu is cached and rebuilt when
categories or products change.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload megamenu.ocmod.zip and click the green + button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install "Mega Menu".
3. Edit: turn Status on, choose a featured product per category if you like, and save.

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 with the default theme.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-mega-menu/
