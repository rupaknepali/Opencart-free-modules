Product Tabs for OpenCart 4
===========================
Add tabs such as Shipping & Returns, Warranty, Size & Fit or Care Instructions to product pages.
Write a tab once (rich text, every language) and show it on all products, on the products of
chosen categories (subcategories included), or on chosen products. {name}, {model} and {brand}
in the content are replaced with each product's own. Tabs appear after Description,
Specification and Reviews; themes without tabs get them as sections.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload producttabs.ocmod.zip and click the green + button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install "Product Tabs".
3. Edit: turn Status on, save, and click + to add a tab.

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-custom-product-tabs/
