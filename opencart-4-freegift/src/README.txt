Free Gift with Purchase for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
Adds a chosen product to the cart for free (quantity 1) when the cart subtotal reaches an amount you set,
and removes it again when the cart drops below it. Optional customer groups and start/end dates, and a
"Spend $12 more and get a free ..." message on the cart page.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload freegift.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
Free Gift with Purchase > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-free-gift-with-purchase/
