Store Pickup for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-store-pickup/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
Click & collect from several pickup locations. Each location is its own option at checkout, with its
address, opening hours, a "ready in" time and a Google Maps link. The location is saved on the order,
and the pickup details appear in the order confirmation email and on the customer's order page.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload storepickup.ocmod.zip, click Install), then
Extensions > Extensions > choose Shipping: install Store Pickup, Edit, add your locations,
turn Status on and save.
No core files are changed (OpenCart events only).
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
