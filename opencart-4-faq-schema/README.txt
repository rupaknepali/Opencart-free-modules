FAQ with Schema for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-faq-schema/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
Questions and answers on product pages (an FAQ tab), category pages and information pages,
as an accessible <details> accordion, with FAQPage JSON-LD structured data.
An FAQ can belong to one page or to every page of a type (e.g. a shipping question on all products).

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload faqschema.ocmod.zip, click Install), then
Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install FAQ with Schema, Edit, turn Status on and save.
Add FAQs with the + button on the same page.
Uninstalling keeps the oc_faq and oc_faq_description tables.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
