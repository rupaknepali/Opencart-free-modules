Product Quick Edit for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
Edit product prices, quantities and the on/off status right in Catalog > Products: click a value, type,
press Enter. Needs "modify" permission for Catalog > Products. Products with variants (and variants)
are left to the full product form, so OpenCart's variant syncing isn't bypassed.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload quickedit.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
Product Quick Edit > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-quick-edit/
