Order Tracking for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-order-tracking-numbers/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
Adds carrier tracking numbers to orders. On Sales > Orders > (order) pick the carrier, paste the
tracking number and click Add tracking: the number is saved, an order history entry with the
tracking link is added and the customer is emailed (OpenCart's own order history email).
Customers see a "Track package" button in My Account > Order History > (order).

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload ordertracking.ocmod.zip, click Install), then
Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Order Tracking, Edit, turn Status on and save.
Carriers and their tracking links are editable; {tracking} is replaced with the number.
Uninstalling keeps the oc_order_tracking table so tracking numbers aren't lost.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
