Product Badges for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-badges/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3). Written for 4.0.2.x and 4.1.x.

Adds Sale (-20%), New, Bestseller and Sold out badges to product cards (category, search, specials,
modules) and, optionally, to the main image on product pages.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload productbadges.ocmod.zip, then click the green Install button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Product Badges (this registers three events), then Edit,
   choose the badges you want, turn Status on and save.
Nothing to add to layouts.

Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
