Coupon Links for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-coupon-links/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
Apply a coupon from a link: https://www.example.com/?coupon=SAVE10 (works on any store URL).
The coupon must exist in Marketing > Coupons and be enabled, and the Coupon order total must be enabled.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload couponlink.ocmod.zip, click Install), then Extensions > Extensions > Modules:
install Coupon Links, Edit, turn Status on and save.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
