Request a Quote for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-request-a-quote/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
Adds a "Request a quote" button and form to product pages: on every product, or only on products priced 0
("price on request", where Add to Cart is replaced). Requests are saved, emailed to the store, and listed in
Sales > Quote Requests.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload requestquote.ocmod.zip, click Install), then Extensions > Extensions > Modules:
install Request a Quote (creates oc_quote_request and adds the menu item), Edit, turn Status on and save.
Uninstalling keeps the table. To remove it: DROP TABLE oc_quote_request;
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
