Recent Sales Notifications for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
A small popup with products from real recent orders ("Organic Coffee Beans was bought 2 hours ago")
and a "Bought 12 times in the last 7 days" line on product pages. Only real orders in the statuses you
choose are used; no names are shown, and the buyer's city only if you switch it on.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload recentsales.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
Recent Sales Notifications > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-recent-sales-notifications/
