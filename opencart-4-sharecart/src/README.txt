Share Cart for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
Adds a "Share this cart" button under the cart. It creates a short link; opening the link adds the same
products, quantities and options to the visitor's cart. Useful for sales reps, B2B quotes, gift lists
and customer support. Prices are never part of the link: the visitor pays current prices.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload sharecart.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
Share Cart > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-share-cart-link/
