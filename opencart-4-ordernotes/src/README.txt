Staff Order Notes for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
Private notes for your team on every order: who wrote what and when, a pinned note
shown at the top of the order, and a note badge in the order list. Customers never see them.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload ordernotes.ocmod.zip
         Extensions > Extensions > Modules > Staff Order Notes > Install, then Edit and enable.
Notes are kept when you uninstall; delete the table oc_ordernote yourself if you want them gone.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-staff-order-notes/
