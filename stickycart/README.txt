Sticky Add to Cart for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-sticky-add-to-cart/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
A bar with the product name, price and Add to Cart that slides up once the main Add to Cart button scrolls out of view.
Products with required options scroll the shopper back to the options.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload stickycart.ocmod.zip, click Install), then Extensions > Extensions > Modules:
install Sticky Add to Cart, Edit, turn Status on and save.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
