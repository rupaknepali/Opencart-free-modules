Product Questions & Answers for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-questions-answers/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3). Written for 4.0.2.x and 4.1.x.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload productqa.ocmod.zip, then click the green Install button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Product Questions & Answers (this creates the oc_product_question table and adds a Catalog menu item).
3. Edit > Settings: turn Status on and save.
4. Design > Layouts > Product: add Product Questions & Answers to Content Bottom.

Questions stay hidden until you answer and publish them in Catalog > Product Questions.
Uninstalling keeps the questions table, so a reinstall doesn't lose answers. To remove it: DROP TABLE oc_product_question;
