Delivery Date and Time Slots for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
Customers choose a delivery day and time slot in checkout. Set lead time, a same-day cutoff,
closed weekdays and dates, and how many orders each slot takes. The slot is saved with the order and
shown in the confirmation email, the customer's order page and the admin order page.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload deliveryslot.ocmod.zip
         Extensions > Extensions > Modules > Delivery Date and Time Slots > Install, then Edit and enable.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-delivery-date-time-slot/
