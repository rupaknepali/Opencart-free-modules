Pre-order for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-pre-order/

Install: Extensions > Installer > upload preorder.ocmod.zip > install (+).
Then Extensions > Extensions > Modules > Pre-order > install, edit, turn Status on and save.
Switch pre-order on per product in Catalog > Products > edit > Pre-order tab.
No core files are changed. Uninstalling removes the events and the startup action and keeps the tables.
