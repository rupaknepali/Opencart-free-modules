Frequently Bought Together for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-frequently-bought-together/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3). Written for 4.0.2.x and 4.1.x.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload boughttogether.ocmod.zip, then click the green Install button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Frequently Bought Together, then Edit and turn Status on.
3. Design > Layouts > Product: add Frequently Bought Together to Content Bottom and save.

Suggestions come from your own orders with a "processing" or "complete" status
(System > Settings > Option). A product page shows the block once another product
shares at least "Minimum shared orders" orders with it. Results are cached for an hour.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
