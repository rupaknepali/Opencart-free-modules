Product Image Zoom for OpenCart 4 (4.0.2.x - 4.1.x)
Hover zoom on the main product image (mouse only), thumbnails that switch the main image, and a
popup that shows a large version of each photo instead of the small popup size.
Upload product photos of 1000 px or more for zoom to be useful.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload imagezoom.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions >
Modules > Product Image Zoom > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-image-zoom/
