Announcement Bar for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-announcement-bar/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
A message bar across the top of every storefront page, with optional link, colours, start/end dates and a close button.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload announcebar.ocmod.zip, click Install), then Extensions > Extensions > Modules:
install Announcement Bar, Edit, enter the message, turn Status on and save.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
