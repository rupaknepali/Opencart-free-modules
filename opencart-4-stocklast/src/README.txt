Out of Stock Last for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
Shows out-of-stock products (quantity 0 or less) after the products in stock on category pages,
search results, brand pages and product modules, or hides them from those lists. Sorting happens in
the database query, so it works across every page of a category, with any sort order the shopper picks.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload stocklast.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
Out of Stock Last > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-out-of-stock-products-last/
