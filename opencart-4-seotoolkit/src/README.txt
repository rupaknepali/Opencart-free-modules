SEO Toolkit for OpenCart 4
==========================
One extension for the SEO work every OpenCart store needs:

- SEO audit: missing SEO URL keywords (per store and language), empty, long and duplicate
  meta titles and descriptions, thin descriptions and products without images, with links
  to the pages to fix.
- Generate missing SEO keywords in bulk (transliterated, unique, subcategories include their
  parent like OpenCart's form does).
- Meta templates: "{name} | {store}", "Buy {name}[ for {price}] at {store}. {description}" and
  more, for products, categories, information pages and brands. Used on the storefront when
  a page's own fields are empty (or always), and "Fill empty meta tags" writes them in.
- Robots noindex for search results, sorted/filtered copies, page 2+ and private pages.
- Structured data: Organization and WebSite (site search) on the home page, BreadcrumbList on
  product, category, information, brand and special pages.
- robots.txt editor with recommended rules.
- In the product, category, information and brand forms, empty SEO keyword and meta title
  fields fill in as you type the name.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload seotoolkit.ocmod.zip and click the green + button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install "SEO Toolkit".
3. Edit: Settings tab, turn Status on and save. Then work through the SEO audit.

Uninstalling removes the events and settings; SEO keywords, meta tags and robots.txt it wrote stay.

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-seo-toolkit/
