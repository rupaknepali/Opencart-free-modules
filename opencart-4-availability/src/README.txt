Product Availability on Product Cards for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-availability-on-product-cards/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3). Converted from the OpenCart 3 version.
Shows In Stock / quantity / stock status on every product card.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload availability.ocmod.zip, click Install), then
Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Product Availability on Product Cards, Edit, turn Status on and save.
No core files are changed (OpenCart events only).
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
