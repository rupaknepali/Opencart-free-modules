Sale Countdown Timer for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-sale-countdown-timer/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).

Shows "Sale ends in 2d 04:12:33" under the price of products whose special price has an end date,
on the product page and (optionally) on product cards.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload salecountdown.ocmod.zip, then click the green Install button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Sale Countdown Timer, then Edit, turn Status on and save.
Then give a special price an End Date (Catalog > Products > Discount tab, a row with Special ticked and Date End set).
