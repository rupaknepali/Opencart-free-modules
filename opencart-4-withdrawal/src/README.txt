EU Withdrawal Button for OpenCart 4 (tested on 4.1.0.4)
The online withdrawal function EU consumers must be offered since 19 June 2026 (Article 11a of
Directive 2011/83/EU, added by Directive (EU) 2023/2673): a "Withdraw from contract here" link on every
page, a form that works without login, a "Confirm withdrawal" step, an acknowledgement email with the
content and the date and time of submission, a note in the order history and a list in the admin.

This is a tool, not legal advice. Check your country's rules and your terms.

Install: Extensions > Installer > Upload withdrawal.ocmod.zip, then Extensions > Extensions > Modules >
EU Withdrawal Button > Install and Edit.
Guide: https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-eu-withdrawal-button/
