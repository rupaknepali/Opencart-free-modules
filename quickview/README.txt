Product Quick View for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-product-quick-view/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3). Written for 4.0.2.x and 4.1.x.

Install
1. Extensions > Installer: upload quickview.ocmod.zip, then click the green Install button.
2. Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Product Quick View (this registers two events), then Edit and turn Status on.
Nothing to add to layouts: the button appears on every product card automatically.

Products with required options or subscriptions show "Choose options" in the popup and link to the product page.
Free to use and modify. No attribution required.
