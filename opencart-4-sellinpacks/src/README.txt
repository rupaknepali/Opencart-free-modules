Sell in Packs for OpenCart 4 - free extension by Webocreation
https://webocreation.com/opencart-4-sell-in-packs/

Tested on OpenCart 4.1.0.4 (PHP 8.3).
Sell products in multiples (packs of 6, cases of 12) and set a maximum quantity per order.
- Product page: "Sold in packs of 6" and a quantity box that keeps to whole packs.
- Product cards: Add to Cart adds one pack.
- Cart: a warning for any product in a quantity that isn't allowed, and checkout stays closed until it's fixed.

Install: Extensions > Installer (upload sellinpacks.ocmod.zip, click Install), then
Extensions > Extensions > Modules: install Sell in Packs, Edit, add products, turn Status on and save.
No core files are changed (OpenCart events only).
